## Full refund

If your eSIM has **not** been activated or connected and you request a refund within {{refundDays}} days of purchase, you get an automatic full refund — request it via the in-app chat or by email. The money returns to your original payment method in 5–10 business days.

## No refund after activation

Once the eSIM has connected to a network, wholesale costs have been incurred, so we cannot refund — except where the law requires otherwise.

## Failed installs

If the eSIM never connected, you receive a full refund even past the window — contact support.

## Fault on our side

If a fault on our side is unresolved 24 hours after your report, you choose: a refund of the unused portion or account credit.

Open **Profile → Help → Contact support** (chat), or email {{email}} with your order ID. We respond within 24 hours.
